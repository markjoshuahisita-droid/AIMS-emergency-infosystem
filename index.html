⚠ Daily Safety Reminder

  • Know the nearest emergency exit.
  • Keep hallways and stairs clear.
  • Report hazards immediately.
  • Participate in evacuation drills.
  • Stay calm during emergencies.

🌎 Earthquake Response

During the shaking

  • DROP to your hands and knees.
  • COVER your head under a sturdy desk.
  • HOLD ON until the shaking stops.
  • Stay away from windows and shelves.

After the shaking

  • Evacuate calmly using the stairs.
  • Do not use elevators.
  • Proceed to the designated assembly area.
  • Expect aftershocks and stay alert.
Remember: Drop • Cover • Hold is the safest earthquake procedure.

🔥 Fire Emergency

What to do

  • Activate the nearest fire alarm.
  • Leave the room immediately.
  • Close the door behind you.
  • Stay low if smoke is present.
  • Follow evacuation signs to the assembly area.

PASS Method

  • 🅿 Pull the safety pin.
  • 🅰 Aim at the base of the fire.
  • 🆂 Squeeze the handle.
  • 🆂 Sweep side to side.
Never: Use elevators, panic, or return for personal belongings.

🌊 Flood Safety

Before flooding

  • Monitor weather advisories.
  • Prepare your emergency kit.
  • Charge your mobile phone.

During flooding

  • Move to higher ground.
  • Avoid walking through floodwater.
  • Keep away from electrical wires.
  • Follow evacuation instructions.
Floodwater may contain hidden holes, debris, and live electricity.

🌪 Typhoon Preparedness

Before

  • Monitor PAGASA updates.
  • Secure loose objects.
  • Prepare food, water, flashlight, and power bank.

During

  • Stay inside the building.
  • Keep away from glass windows.
  • Follow school suspension announcements.

After

  • Beware of fallen wires and damaged structures.
  • Wait for official clearance before returning.

❤️ Basic First Aid

Primary Response

  • Ensure the area is safe.
  • Call the school clinic immediately.
  • Check if the person is responsive.
  • Control severe bleeding using direct pressure.
  • Keep the victim calm until help arrives.

Burns

  • Cool with running water for 20 minutes.
  • Cover with a clean cloth.
  • Do not apply toothpaste, oil, or ice.

Fainting

  • Lay the person flat.
  • Elevate the legs slightly.
  • Seek medical assistance.

🚨 Security Incident

RUN • HIDE • REPORT

  • RUN if there is a safe escape route.
  • HIDE by locking or barricading the room.
  • SILENCE your phone while hiding.
  • REPORT the incident when it is safe.
Remain calm and follow instructions from school authorities.
