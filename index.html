Daily Safety Reminder

  • Know your nearest emergency exit.
  • Keep hallways and stairways clear.
  • Report hazards immediately.
  • Participate in evacuation drills.
  • Stay calm during emergencies.

Fire Emergency

What to do

  • Activate the nearest fire alarm.
  • Evacuate immediately using the nearest exit.
  • Stay low if smoke is present.
  • Close doors behind you.
  • Proceed to the designated assembly area.

PASS Method

  • P – Pull the safety pin.
  • A – Aim at the base of the fire.
  • S – Squeeze the handle.
  • S – Sweep side to side.
Never use elevators or return for personal belongings.

Earthquake Response

During the shaking

  • DROP to your hands and knees.
  • COVER your head under a sturdy desk.
  • HOLD ON until shaking stops.
  • Stay away from windows and glass.

After the shaking

  • Evacuate calmly using the stairs.
  • Do not use elevators.
  • Go to the designated assembly area.
  • Prepare for aftershocks.
Remember: Drop • Cover • Hold

Flood Safety

Safety Procedure

  • Move to higher ground immediately.
  • Avoid walking through floodwater.
  • Keep away from electrical wires.
  • Follow evacuation announcements.
  • Wait for official clearance.
Floodwater may contain hidden debris and live electricity.

Basic First Aid

Primary Response

  • Ensure the area is safe.
  • Call the school clinic.
  • Check responsiveness.
  • Control severe bleeding.
  • Keep the victim calm.

For Burns

  • Cool with running water for 20 minutes.
  • Cover with a clean cloth.
  • Do not apply toothpaste or ice.

Typhoon Preparedness

Before

  • Monitor PAGASA weather updates.
  • Prepare emergency supplies.
  • Charge communication devices.

During

  • Stay indoors.
  • Avoid windows and damaged structures.
  • Follow school advisories.

After

  • Beware of fallen power lines.
  • Wait for official clearance.

Security Incident

RUN • HIDE • REPORT

  • RUN if a safe escape route exists.
  • HIDE by locking or barricading the room.
  • Silence your mobile phone.
  • REPORT the incident when it is safe.
Remain calm and follow school authorities.
